# Copyright policy / 版權政策

AI FACE Personas accepts only original characters that can be legally redistributed and modified under the declared license.

AI FACE Personas 只接受能依聲明授權合法修改與再散布的原創角色。

## Immediate rejection / 直接拒絕

Submissions are rejected when they contain recognizable third-party characters, fan art, celebrity likenesses, copied costumes, logos, trademarks, downloaded images, screenshots, or unclear ownership.

若投稿包含可辨識的第三方角色、二創、名人肖像、複製服裝、Logo、商標、下載圖片、截圖或權利不明內容，將直接拒絕。

AI-generated content is reviewed under the same rule. A generator producing the pixels does not prove that the resulting character is original or safe to redistribute.

AI 生成內容適用相同規則。由生成器產生像素，不代表角色必然原創或可以安全再散布。

## Removal / 移除

Maintainers may reject, hide, or remove a character when the rights declaration is incomplete, evidence is inconsistent, or a credible rights concern is raised. Automated validation is not legal clearance.

若權利聲明不完整、資料互相矛盾，或出現可信的權利疑慮，維護者可以拒絕、隱藏或移除角色。自動驗證通過不等於法律許可。

## Reporting a concern / 回報疑慮

For an informal maintainer review, open the repository's copyright report issue form and include:

- The exact GitHub URL of the file.
- The original work or rights holder.
- A public source showing the suspected conflict.
- A short explanation of the concern.

若要請維護者先行檢查，請使用版權檢舉 Issue 表單，提供精確檔案網址、原作品或權利人、可公開查證的來源與簡短說明。

Do not post private addresses, phone numbers, identity documents, contracts, or other sensitive information in a public issue. Formal copyright notices should follow [GitHub's official DMCA process](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/content-removal-policies/guide-to-submitting-a-dmca-takedown-notice).

請勿在公開 Issue 張貼地址、電話、身分文件、合約或其他敏感資訊。正式版權通知應使用 [GitHub 官方 DMCA 程序](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/content-removal-policies/guide-to-submitting-a-dmca-takedown-notice)。

This policy is a project contribution rule, not legal advice.

本政策是專案投稿規則，不是法律意見。
